# Group Bookings

## Group Bookings

- **Minimum Group Size:** 15 jumpers *(Note: Applies to all group bookings)*
- **Discount Code:** GROUPBOOKING25 *(Note: 25% off for 15 or more jumpers)*
- **Deposit Required:** 25% *(Note: Required at time of booking)*
- **120 Min Pricing:** $25.47 per jumper *(Note: 25% discount on $33.89 which is 120 min $29.90 plus socks $3.99)*
- **Weekday vs Weekend Pricing:** Same *(Note: Pricing is the same for weekdays and weekends)*
- **Included:** Jump time plus Aero Socks *(Note: Add-on options available at extra cost)*
- **Go Karting:** Not included in group rate *(Note: Can be added on separately)*
