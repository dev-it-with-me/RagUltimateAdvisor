# Voice Call Scripts

## Greeting

> Thank you for calling AeroSports Scarborough! How can I help you today?

*Note: Use warm, upbeat tone*

## Hold

> One moment while I look that up for you.

*Note: Use when retrieving info*

## Transfer To Human

> Let me connect you with one of our team members who can help you with that. One moment please.

*Note: Use when the question is beyond what the AI can handle*

## Closing

> Thanks for calling AeroSports Scarborough! We look forward to seeing you. Have a great day!

*Note: Always end on a positive note*

## Address Response

> We are at 1120 Birchmount Road in Scarborough. The postal code is M1K 5G4.

*Note: Speak address clearly and slowly*

## Hours Response

> We are open Sunday through Thursday from 10 AM to 8 PM, and Friday and Saturday from 10 AM to 10 PM.

*Note: Say times clearly with AM/PM*

## Price Overview

> Our Premium Jump Pass is $24.90 for 90 minutes, and the VIP Jump Pass is $29.90 for 120 minutes. We also have an All Day Pass for $39.90. All prices are plus tax. Aero Socks are required and cost $3.99 but they are reusable so you can bring them back next time.

*Note: Give a brief summary, offer to go deeper*

## Birthday Overview

> We have three birthday party packages. The Premium Party starts at $39.90 per jumper with 75 minutes of jump time and 45 minutes in a private party room. The VIP Party is $44.90 per jumper with 90 minutes of jump time and 60 minutes of room time. And the Ultimate Party is $59.90 per jumper with 120 minutes of jump time, 60 minutes of room time, plus go karting for every jumper. All packages include pizza and drinks. Minimum 10 jumpers. Would you like more details on any package?

*Note: Pause between packages for clarity*

## Gokart Overview

> We have go karting on two tracks. The main track is for riders 54 inches or taller and costs $19.90 for a standalone race or $9.90 as an add-on if you already have a jump pass. The mini track is for riders 36 inches or taller and costs $9.90 standalone or $4.90 as an add-on. Each race is up to 10 laps. Just remember you need closed-toed shoes for go karting.

*Note: Mention height requirements clearly*

## Booking Redirect

> You can book online at our website aerosportsparks.ca or I can help you with that right now. Would you like to proceed?

*Note: Guide toward booking*

## Waiver Info

> Everyone who enters the park needs a signed waiver, including non-jumpers. You can fill it out online ahead of time at our website to save time at check-in. For anyone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must sign the waiver.

*Note: Emphasize online waiver to reduce wait times*

## Group Booking

> For groups of 15 or more, we offer 25% off with the code GROUPBOOKING25. That brings the price down to about $25.47 per person for 120 minutes including socks. Would you like to book a group visit?

*Note: Emphasize the savings*

## Promo March Bday

> Right now we have a March Birthday Promotion with up to $150 off birthday parties. Use code MARCHBDAY50 for $50 off a Premium party, MARCHBDAY100 for $100 off a VIP party, or MARCHBDAY150 for $150 off an Ultimate party. You need to book by March 31st and host by September 30th.

*Note: Only share if promo status is Active*

## Camp Overview

> We offer AeroCamp for kids ages 4 to 12. Full day camp runs from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM and costs $69.90 per day or $299.90 for a full week. We also have half-day options for $39.90. Activities include trampolines, games, challenges, crafts, and group activities. Early drop-off from 7:30 AM and late pick-up past 6 PM are available for $10 each.

*Note: Mention current camp availability if applicable*

## Cannot Answer

> That is a great question. I do not have that specific information right now, but I can have someone from our team get back to you. Can I get your name and phone number?

*Note: Collect callback info when unsure*
