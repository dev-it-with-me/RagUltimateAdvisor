# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: Are AeroSports jump socks required?**

A: Yes, AeroSports socks are required at all locations. Jump socks are available for $3.99 + tax. They are reusable, so keep yours for future visits!

**Q: Is there a weight limit for the trampolines?**

A: Our trampolines have a 300-pound weight limit.

**Q: Are waivers required? Can they be filled out online?**

A: Waivers are required for every guest to the park including non-jumpers. You can fill out the waiver online before you arrive to reduce check-in time. Children under 18 must have the waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian.

**Q: Can I leave my child(ren) unattended?**

A: Children under 12 cannot be dropped off without an adult present. While every child under 18 needs a parent or legal guardian to sign the waiver, please ensure all children understand the park rules before dropping them off.

**Q: Do spectators have to pay to watch jumpers?**

A: Non-jumpers are never charged admission. Only jumpers pay for general admission.

**Q: Do you have food available to purchase?**

A: Yes! We have a fully stocked Snack Shack serving delicious treats.

**Q: What days are the least busy?**

A: Weekends are our busiest days. Mondays and Tuesdays are usually less busy.

**Q: Can my small child come to the park?**

A: As long as the child can walk, they can jump!

**Q: Do you sell gift cards?**

A: Yes! Purchase a physical gift card at the park or buy one online.

**Q: Do jump cards expire?**

A: No, our jump cards never expire and can be used by different jumpers.

**Q: When are refunds processed?**

A: Refunds take 3-5 business days to reflect in your bank account.

**Q: What attractions do you have in the park?**

A: We have trampoines, ninja warrior obstacle course, ninja maze, donut slide, 360 bike, Go Karting, dodgeball area in trampoline, rock walls, airbags.

**Q: What is included in the jump pass price?**

A: Your jump pass includes access to all attractions in the park like trampolines, dodgeball, rock walls, ninja course, slam basketball, 360 bicycle, slides, jump tower, and ninja maze. Go karting is the only attraction not included and needs a separate ticket or add-on.

**Q: What is the address of AeroSports Scarborough?**

A: We are located at 1120 Birchmount Rd, Scarborough, Ontario, M1K 5G4. You can find us on Google Maps by searching AeroSports Scarborough.

**Q: What are your hours of operation?**

A: We are open Sunday to Thursday from 10am to 8pm, and Friday to Saturday from 10am to 10pm.

**Q: How do I book online?**

A: You can book your jump passes online at ecom.roller.app/aerosportsscarborough. For birthday parties, visit the birthday booking page on our website or call us at 289-454-5555.

**Q: Do you have any current promotions or deals?**

A: Check our website or ask us about current promotions. We run monthly specials including birthday party discounts and seasonal deals.

**Q: Is there a special needs program?**

A: Yes! We offer a special needs program during all operating hours for $19.90. It includes 120 minutes of play time. One support worker is admitted free with a signed waiver. Go kart races are not included.

**Q: What is glow night at Scarborough?**

A: At AeroSports Scarborough, we have glow in the dark all the time during regular operating hours. The neon lights and glow effects are always on, so you can enjoy the glow experience any time you visit at no extra cost.

**Q: Can I host a corporate event or team building activity?**

A: Yes! We offer corporate and team party packages perfect for team building, milestone celebrations, and employee rewards. Call us at 289-454-5555 or email events.scb@aerosportsparks.ca to customize your event.

**Q: Can schools book field trips at AeroSports?**

A: Absolutely! We welcome school groups, church groups, sports teams, and youth organizations. Minimum 15 people. Call 289-454-5555 or email events.scb@aerosportsparks.ca to book.

**Q: Can I rent the whole facility for a private event?**

A: Yes, we offer full facility rentals. Weekday rentals are $2,000 per hour and weekend rentals are $3,000 per hour. Capacity is up to 220 jumpers with exclusive access to the entire facility including unlimited go karting.

## Go Karting

**Q: Do you have single and dual karts?**

A: Yes! We have a mini track for kids as young as 3, and a big track with both single and dual rider karts.

**Q: What's the minimum height requirement for go karting?**

A: Mini Track: 36 inches. Main Track (driver): 54 inches. Dual Kart (passenger): 36 inches.

**Q: Do I need a jump pass for go karting?**

A: No! You can buy standalone go karting races without a jump pass.

**Q: Do both driver and passenger need a race pass?**

A: Yes, every customer participating in go karting needs a valid race pass.

**Q: Are AeroSports socks required for go karting?**

A: Closed-toed shoes are required on the track. AeroSports socks are required for all other attractions.

**Q: Can I book a specific time slot for go karting?**

A: Yes you can, if you haven't booked online, it will be first come first served, but you can book a time slot in person as well.

**Q: What is Glow?**

A: At Scarborough, we have glow in the dark at ALL times! The main arena features laser lights and LED lights for an amazing experience.

**Q: How tall do you have to be for go karting?**

A: For the main track, drivers must be at least 54 inches tall. For the mini track, riders must be at least 36 inches tall. Closed-toed shoes are required for all go kart riders.

**Q: How much does go karting cost?**

A: A standalone go kart race on the main track is $19.90 and the mini track is $9.90. If you already have a jump pass, add-on races are $9.90 for the main track and $4.90 for the mini track. Each race is up to 10 laps.

## Group Booking

**Q: How do I book a group event?**

A: Book through our website or by phone. A 25% deposit is required. Confirm within the holding period to avoid losing your reservation.

**Q: Is a jumpers list required?**

A: Yes, provide the jumpers list two weeks before the booking date so we can pre-check waivers and speed up check-in.

**Q: Is pricing the same for weekdays and weekends?**

A: Group jump pricing is the same for weekdays and weekends. Facility rental rates differ.

**Q: What's included in the group jump?**

A: Jump time and Aero socks are included. Add-on options available at extra cost.

**Q: What is the minimum number of jumpers?**

A: 15 jumpers minimum to qualify for group event pricing.

**Q: Are private rooms included with group events?**

A: Rooms are not included but can be rented for an additional cost.

**Q: What's included in the facility rental?**

A: Complimentary jump socks, exclusive facility access (up to 220 jumpers), all party rooms, tablecloths and cutlery. Unlimited go karting. Food/catering available at additional cost.

**Q: How do I prepare for my group event?**

A: Confirm booking and pay 25% deposit. Submit jumpers list two weeks before. Ensure all waivers signed in advance. Stay in touch with our team.

**Q: What is the cancellation or refund policy?**

A: Deposits are non-refundable. Rescheduling options may be available. Contact our team.

**Q: Are food and catering options available?**

A: Yes, food and catering can be arranged at additional cost.

**Q: What's the discount code for group events?**

A: Use code GROUPBOOKING25 for 25% off group rates for 15+ jumpers.

**Q: Is go karting included in group bookings?**

A: Yes! 1x go kart race per jumper for group bookings. Unlimited go karting for facility rentals.

## Birthday Party

**Q: How early should guests arrive?**

A: All guests should arrive 20 minutes before the scheduled jump time for check-in.

**Q: Are socks included in the party package?**

A: Sock inclusions vary by package. Premium: No. VIP: 50% off. Ultimate: FREE socks.

**Q: Can I invite additional jumpers beyond the minimum?**

A: Yes, additional jumpers can be added at the per-jumper price. Ensure total doesn't exceed room capacity.

**Q: What if I forget to pre-order add-ons?**

A: Add-ons can be ordered within 24 hours but will incur a $20 late ordering fee.

**Q: Are attractions exclusive to my party?**

A: No, attractions are shared with other patrons. Your party room is private.

**Q: Can I extend the jumping or party room time?**

A: Additional time may be available for purchase based on availability.

**Q: What if some guests don't show up?**

A: The minimum jumper count for your package is still charged. Additional fees for extra arrivals.

**Q: Can I request a specific party room?**

A: We try to accommodate but cannot guarantee — based on availability.

**Q: Is there an area for parents to wait?**

A: Yes, parents can relax in our viewing area or common spaces.

**Q: Can I bring outside decorations?**

A: Yes, as long as they comply with policies. No confetti, piñatas, or silly string. $40 cleaning charge if used.

**Q: What is the check-in process for party guests?**

A: Staff assists with check-in. Ensure waivers completed beforehand. Wristbands provided.

**Q: Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?**

A: Yes, with advance notice. Contact us to discuss options.

**Q: What if my party runs late?**

A: Parties must follow scheduled time. Overages may result in additional fees.

**Q: Is the birthday child included in the jumper count?**

A: Yes, the birthday child counts toward the minimum.

**Q: Are party invitations provided?**

A: Digital invitations included in every package. Physical invitations: $0.75 each.

**Q: Can I bring themed props or costumes?**

A: Yes, as long as they are safe and suitable for jumping.

**Q: What if my child gets injured?**

A: Trained staff respond on-site. Parents notified immediately. Emergency protocols for serious injuries.

**Q: Can I tour the facility before booking?**

A: Yes! Contact us to arrange a visit.

**Q: Can I split the party cost?**

A: Yes, payments can be split. Let us know in advance.

**Q: Do you help with setup and cleanup?**

A: Yes, staff assists with setup and handles cleanup.

**Q: Are there options for non-jumpers?**

A: Non-jumpers can enjoy the party room and food but not attractions. Don't count toward minimum.

**Q: Can I book for twins or multiple children?**

A: Yes! Let us know in advance so we can customize the plans.

**Q: What is the cancellation/rescheduling policy?**

A: Reschedule within 1 year, no charge. Cancel 14+ days: store gift card. Within 14 days: no refund.

**Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my package?**

A: Must be 14+ days in advance, subject to availability.

**Q: Are there rewards for hosting multiple parties?**

A: Contact us about repeat booking discounts or rewards.

## Aero Camp

**Q: What's included in Aero Camp?**

A: Supervised trampoline activities, games, challenges, crafts, contests, and group activities.

**Q: Can I book individual days or must I book a full week?**

A: You can book individual days or a full week. Both pricing options available.

**Q: Is lunch included?**

A: Lunch is not included but can be added for $9.90/day. You can also send a packed lunch.

**Q: How do I register my child?**

A: Register online or call the park. Spots fill up quickly — early registration recommended.

**Q: What should my child bring?**

A: Comfortable activewear, packed lunch/snacks (if not buying lunch combo), refillable water bottle.

**Q: Is there supervision during camp?**

A: Yes, trained staff supervise at all times.

**Q: Can I add early drop-off or late pick-up last minute?**

A: Yes, typically can be added with short notice. Notify camp team ASAP.

**Q: What is the age requirement?**

A: Campers must be 4-12 years old.

**Q: Are waivers required for camp?**

A: Yes, signed waiver required before attending. Complete online or at the park.

**Q: When are PA Day Camps offered?**

A: Available on all PA days per Catholic and Public Elementary School Boards. Check website or call.

**Q: When is Summer Camp?**

A: Runs during specific summer weeks. Dates announced closer to start.

**Q: When is March Break Camp?**

A: During official March Break for all elementary and secondary students.

**Q: When is Winter Camp?**

A: During winter holiday period. Dates posted closer to season.

**Q: Can parents stay during camp?**

A: We encourage drop-off for a smooth experience. Let us know if your child needs settling-in support.

**Q: How are extended hours structured?**

A: Supervised free play or quiet activities. Early drop-off from 7:30 AM. Late pick-up past 6PM (prices vary).

**Q: Is there a lost and found?**

A: Yes. Please label all personal items.

**Q: Are electronic devices allowed?**

A: We recommend leaving them at home to avoid distractions or damage.

**Q: Are there rest periods during the day?**

A: Yes, short breaks for rest, snacks, and lunch. Quiet activities like crafts may be offered.

**Q: What if my child feels unwell?**

A: We notify you immediately. Trained staff handle minor first-aid. Provide emergency contacts at registration.

**Q: How are behavioral issues handled?**

A: Staff manage conflicts calmly. Parents informed if further action needed.

**Q: What is included in the lunch combo?**

A: Kid-friendly options: pizza, a drink, and a snack.

**Q: Can my child join if they miss the first day?**

A: Yes, late arrivals welcome if space available.
