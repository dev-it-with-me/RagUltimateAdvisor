# Chatbot Quick Replies

## What To Do

**Sample question:** What can I do at AeroSports Scarborough?

**Response:** AeroSports Scarborough has tons of activities! We have wall-to-wall trampolines, a ninja warrior course, ninja maze, rock climbing walls, dodgeball, slam basketball, a 360 bicycle ride, giant slides, a jump tower, and go karting. Everything except go karting is included in your jump pass.

## How Much

**Sample question:** How much does it cost?

**Response:** A 90-minute Premium Jump Pass is $24.90 and a 120-minute VIP Jump Pass is $29.90. The All Day Pass is $39.90. AeroSports grip socks are required and cost $3.99 but are reusable. All prices are plus tax. Go karting is separate starting at $9.90. Would you like to book?

## What Age

**Sample question:** What age can kids come?

**Response:** All ages are welcome! As long as your child can walk, they can jump. Kids under 2 get in free with a paying adult. We also have Toddler Time on Saturday mornings from 9 to 10 AM exclusively for ages 5 and under at $15.90, and one parent jumps free.

## Party Info

**Sample question:** Tell me about birthday parties

**Response:** We have 3 birthday packages starting at $39.90 per jumper with a minimum of 10 kids. All packages include pizza, drinks, a private party room, a glow t-shirt for the birthday child, and digital invitations. The Ultimate Package also includes go karting, free 30 day pass and free socks for everyone! Want details on a specific package?

## Directions

**Sample question:** Where are you located?

**Response:** We are at 1120 Birchmount Road in Scarborough, ON M1K 5G4. You can find us on Google Maps by searching AeroSports Scarborough. Our phone number is 289-454-5555.

## Safety

**Sample question:** Is it safe for my kids?

**Response:** Safety is our top priority. We have trained staff, park rules posted throughout, and a 300 pound weight limit on trampolines. All jumpers need a signed waiver. Kids under 10 must have an adult present. Spectators are always free and we have comfy seating and free Wi-Fi for parents.
