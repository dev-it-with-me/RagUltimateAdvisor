# Birthday Parties

## Premium Party

The Premium Party is $39.90 per jumper (minimum 10 jumpers, base price $399). Includes 75 minutes on the trampoline courts followed by 45 minutes in a private party room (fits 25-30 people). Food included: 1 large pizza per 5 jumpers and 1 juice box per jumper. The birthday child gets a FREE glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a FREE 60-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a FREE 60-minute add-on pass. Go karting is NOT included but can be added on for $9.90 per person. No socks discount, no physical invitations, and outside food requires a $50 fee. Total party duration is approximately 2 hours (75 min jump + 45 min room).

## VIP Party

The VIP Party is $44.90 per jumper (minimum 10 jumpers, base price $449). Includes 90 minutes on the trampoline courts followed by 60 minutes in a private party room (fits 25-30 people). Food included: 1 large pizza per 5 jumpers, plus 1 juice box AND 1 water bottle per jumper. The birthday child gets a FREE glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a FREE 60-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a FREE 60-minute add-on pass. Socks are 50% off ($2.00 instead of $3.99). Go karting is NOT included but can be added on for $9.90 per person. No physical invitations included, and outside food requires a $50 fee. Total party duration is approximately 2.5 hours (90 min jump + 60 min room).

## Ultimate Party

The Ultimate Party is $59.90 per jumper (minimum 10 jumpers, base price $599). Includes 120 minutes on the trampoline courts followed by 60 minutes in a private party room (fits 25-30 people). Food included: 1 large pizza per 5 jumpers, plus 1 juice box OR pop and 1 water bottle per jumper. The birthday child gets a FREE glow t-shirt, digital AND physical invitations, and a FREE 30-Day jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a FREE 60-minute add-on pass. Socks are FREE for all jumpers. Go karting is INCLUDED (1 race per jumper). The $50 outside food fee is waived. Total party duration is approximately 3 hours (120 min jump + 60 min room). This is the most complete package with go karting, free socks, and the best perks for the birthday child.

## All Packages

AeroSports Scarborough offers 3 birthday party packages: Premium ($39.90/jumper), VIP ($44.90/jumper), and Ultimate ($59.90/jumper). All require a minimum of 10 jumpers. All include pizza, drinks, a private party room, a glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a FREE 60-min add-on pass for guest jumpers.

**What Upgrades From Premium To Vip:** Upgrading from Premium to VIP adds: 15 more minutes of jump time (90 vs 75), 15 more minutes of room time (60 vs 45), a water bottle in addition to the juice box, and 50% off socks ($2.00 instead of $3.99). Cost difference is $5 per jumper ($50 more for 10 jumpers).

**What Upgrades From Vip To Ultimate:** Upgrading from VIP to Ultimate adds: 30 more minutes of jump time (120 vs 90), go karting included for every jumper, FREE socks for all jumpers, FREE physical invitations, a FREE 30-Day pass for the birthday child (instead of 60-min), the $50 outside food fee is waived, and drinks include pop as an option. Cost difference is $15 per jumper ($150 more for 10 jumpers).

**Best Value:** The Ultimate Party offers the most value with go karting alone worth $9.90/jumper ($99 for 10 kids), plus free socks ($3.99/jumper = $39.90 for 10), and the waived $50 outside food fee. That is roughly $189 in extras for $150 more than VIP.
