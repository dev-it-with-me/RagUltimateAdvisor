## Terms and Conditions

All jumpers are required to wear
Aerosports Socks
on all park attractions.
Stand-Alone Go Kart Tickets are applicable if you are NOT purchasing a jump pass.
Go Kart Add-Ons are applicable if you HAVE already purchased a jump pass.
Closed-toed shoes are required for Go Karting.
Height restrictions apply for Go Karting.
Adults or individuals who are staying to watch their kids or supervise are counted as
spectators
.
Toddlers under 2 must have a parent with them who has purchased an
adult ticket (Ages 6+)
.
All purchases are final and non-refundable. However, rescheduling is free of charge and can be altered only
24 hours*
prior to the reservation date.
*Does not apply to Birthday party schedule changes.
Prices shown are
plus HST
.
All jumpers must have a completed
liability waiver
on file in order to jump. Anyone under 18 must have the waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian.
Anyone 18 or older can sign for themselves with a
valid photo ID
(must include picture and date of birth). Fill out the waiver online before arriving (call to confirm proper completion) or complete it on site.
Parents, unwind while your kids jump and play! Enjoy
complimentary Wi-Fi
and grab a snack at the
Snack Shack
. We offer plenty of seating throughout the facility—perfect for relaxing while your little ones burn off energy.
